★ STEPS

Make it happen in
just four steps

We've made the entire process smooth and hassle-free. Our team guides you through every step from booking to arrival — just show up ready for waves.

01

Choose your package

We have three options: Neighbor (twin room with 2 single beds), Double (king bed for couples or solo travelers), and Squad (group suite for friends). Pick what fits your budget and travel style.

02

Leave your contact details

Fill out a quick form with your name, email, surf level, and preferred package. Our manager will reach out within 24 hours to answer all your questions, confirm availability, and guide you through the next steps.

03

Complete payment

Once everything is confirmed, secure your spot with payment. You'll receive a welcome pack with all camp details and a packing list, and be added to your camp WhatsApp group to meet everyone before departure.

04

Get ready to go

We'll send arrival instructions and arrange airport pickup. Just bring yourself and good vibes — we'll take care of everything else. See you in Canggu!

★ FAQ

Frequently
Asked Questions

👋

Still have questions?

Whether it's about the program, accommodation, or anything in between — we're happy to help.

Contact Us
01

Do I need to know how to surf already?

Not at all! Most people who come are complete beginners or have only surfed a few times. Our instructors work with all levels, and we split into groups so you're learning with people at your pace. If you can swim and are willing to try, you're ready.

02

I'm traveling alone — will I feel out of place?

Over 70% of our guests travel solo! From shared villa breakfasts to group surf sessions and night beach bonfires, our retreat is designed to build lifelong friendships effortlessly.

03

What's not included in the price?

Flights, travel insurance, personal alcoholic beverages, and dinner on two open nights are not included. All daily breakfasts, lunches, coaching, equipment, and airport transfers are fully covered.

04

What if the dates don't work for me?

We run weekly retreat cycles throughout the season. Contact our team to check custom arrival dates or join our waitlist for upcoming pop-up sessions.

05

Is Bali safe for travelers?

Yes, Bali is widely known as one of the safest travel destinations in the world. Our villa is located in a private gated compound with 24/7 security and dedicated staff.
