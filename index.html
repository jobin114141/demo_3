Learn to ride waves with Bali's
best coaches

Our instructors are Bali locals who learned to surf on these waves as kids. They know which break suits your level and exactly what you need to hear — whether it's your first wave or you're working on cutbacks.

They adapt to you. Total beginner? They'll walk you through every step. Already surfing? They'll push your technique further. By the end of two weeks, you won't just trust them—you'll genuinely miss surfing with them.

ISA-Certified Pros

All instructors hold international surf certification with 5+ years teaching experience.

Small Group Focus
